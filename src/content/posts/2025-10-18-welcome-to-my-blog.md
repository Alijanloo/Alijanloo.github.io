# Hello there! Welcome to my blog

I know the era of nerdy blogging might be over, but I like to see this space as a tool for sharing experiences and technical knowledge. Until now, I mostly used Notion and similar tools for taking notes on topics related to technology, artificial intelligence, etc.—but all of it was in private environments, not online.

Here, I plan to make some of those notes public; maybe one day they’ll be useful to someone, and it’s also a step toward building a personal brand.

---

## About the topics I write about here
- Technology, programming, artificial intelligence 🤖
- Book summaries and reviews of what I’ve learned 📚
- Thought notes: philosophy, psychology, politics 🧩
- Personal conclusions and career experiences

> Probably not many people will check this place, but just publishing issues that make up a big part of my personal life in a public space is enough to satisfy my sense of *Self-Disclosure*.
